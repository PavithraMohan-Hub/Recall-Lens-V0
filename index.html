Strict relevance gate

An item is core evidence only when a remembered visual item, incomplete or uncertain cues, and a retrieval attempt or barrier are present.

Out of scope

Known limitations

Selection bias

Public posters skew toward problems and highly engaged users.

Prevalence unknown

Evidence counts are not percentages of Google Photos users.

Platform effects

Moderation and discoverability shape which conversations are visible.

AI fallibility

Ambiguous stories can be miscoded; low confidence requires review.
